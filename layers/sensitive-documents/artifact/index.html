🔒 Sensitive Documents
🔒 Principal-only · re-authenticate (step-up) to open
Your most sensitive paperwork — EIN letters, tax returns and forms (1099 / W-2 / W-4 / I-9 / K-1), Social-Security/TIN pages, and owner ID. These are limited to cleared principals, and opening one asks you to re-authenticate first. They surface on their related record (a vendor, an entity, a person) only as a locked reference — never as readable content — until you unlock them here.
Uploads go straight into Foundation Layer’s private, principal-only store. A tester or team member without financial clearance can’t see these at all. Opening a document requires a fresh sign-in (step-up) — if you haven’t re-authenticated recently you’ll be asked to.
