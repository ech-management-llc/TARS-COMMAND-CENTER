🏗️ Construction Files
Blueprints, drawings, renderings, permits, and specs for your builds — stored once and surfaced wherever they’re relevant (a blueprint on the Property File, a quote on the Deal). Plans and permits are readable by your crew; cost documents (quotes, takeoffs, change orders) are limited to cleared principals.
Uploads are stored in Foundation Layer’s private document store and attach to this Construction Files category. To surface a file on a specific Property, Deal, or work order, use the “linked documents” strip on that record (same store, one link).
