ROLES
Owner — full control. · Admin / Staff (e.g. Ashley) — most layers; external sends gated to draft-for-approval. · Read-only / Viewer — see, can't change. · Tenant (reserved) — their unit only. · Field (e.g. Construction Supervisor) — signs in by phone or email (magic-link / SMS), scoped to ONE layer/job.
Role + entitlement together decide what renders. Per-tenant isolation: a tenant only ever sees their own org's layers + data.
