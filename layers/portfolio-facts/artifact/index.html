📐 Portfolio Facts
The numbers Plaid can't see — entered once, computed forever. Asset value, loan balance, and door count per entity. These are the missing inputs for LTV and per-door cash flow; the moment you save a row, the Financial dashboard computes those rules live for that entity. Nothing is averaged or guessed — anything you leave blank stays honestly UNKNOWN.
HOW TO ENTER — BLANK vs ZERO
• Leave blank = you don't know it yet → stays UNKNOWN on the dashboard (never guessed).
• Enter 0 / tick Debt-free = a real zero (e.g. a paid-off, debt-free property).
Why it matters: leaving a loan blank does not mean $0 — it means unknown. Only a real debt-free property is $0. This keeps a low LTV from being faked by an omitted loan.
